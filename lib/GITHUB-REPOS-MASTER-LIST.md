**Status means:** An accessible page does not mean installed, integrated, usable without a service, licensed for every asset, or tested. Recheck environment and dependencies in each new session. Repo code, models, datasets, music and APIs can have separate terms. A 404 may mean renamed, private or removed.

**User's direction:** Personal Indian creator/brand reels first. Combine suitable complete systems where useful; never limit a repo to one or two files by default. Inspect the entire structure, dependencies, safety and licenses, then use/clone/install automatically as needed. Keep Drive link-first and workspace light. Plan → user approval → render. Do not claim integration before testing.

**Target workflow:** Real reference + research → reel analyzer and Instagram skills → original Hindi/Hinglish hook/script/storyboard → real browser demo/assets → HyperFrames and Remotion graphics/edit → captions/audio → playback quality review → deliver.
